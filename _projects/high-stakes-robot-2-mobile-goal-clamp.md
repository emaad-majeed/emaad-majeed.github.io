This is the mobile goal clamp I built for High Stakes Robot 2, my VEX V5 competition robot for team 38535C. It uses a bright green plastic clamp plate mounted low at the back of the chassis that closes onto a mobile goal so the robot can carry it around the field, and one photo shows the yellow goal post seated against the clamp. The close photos show the clamp plate bolted to the metal frame and the mechanism next to the pneumatic tanks that actuate it.
