This was the very first robot that I built. The preroller is 11W and is chain driven from the motor attached to it, and the conveyor is 11W. The conveyor is attached to a 4 bar lift powered by two 11W red motors. It could score wall stakes, alliance stakes, and mobile goals, and it could hang.
