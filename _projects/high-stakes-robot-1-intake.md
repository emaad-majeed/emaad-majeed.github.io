This is the intake I built for High Stakes Robot 1, my VEX V5 competition robot for team 38535C. The mechanism sits at the front of the base and uses a chain driven roller running up an angled metal arm to grab rings off the field and carry them upward. This photo shows the robot on the practice field with the intake raised over the tread base and rings scattered around it.
