This is the intake I built for High Stakes Robot 1, my first VEX V5 competition robot. The preroller is 11W and is chain driven from the motor attached to it, and the conveyor is 11W. The conveyor sits on a 4 bar lift powered by two 11W red motors. That conveyor lift was for scoring wall stakes, and it was also a concept for climbing the tower.
