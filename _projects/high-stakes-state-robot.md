This is the complete VEX V5 robot I designed and built for the High Stakes state competition, running under team number 85535C. It combines a drivetrain, an intake, a mobile goal setup, and scoring mechanisms into one machine, using an aluminum structure with several laser cut and printed plastic brackets. The photos show the assembled robot on the build bench during final tuning.
