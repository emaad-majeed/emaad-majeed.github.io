This is the center strip of the overhead in my homemade 737 cockpit. The laser cut face groups several sections together, including the CIRCUT BREAKER knobs, EQUIP COOLING supply and exhaust, EMER EXIT LIGHTS, the NO SMOKING and FASTEN BELTS signs, ATTEND and GRD CALL, and the R WIPER selector. I drew every label, knob and switch opening as a laser cut vector before cutting. The second design file is the matching back plate that carries the round and rectangular holes for the same hardware.
