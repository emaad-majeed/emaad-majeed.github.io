This is the pneumatic panel that handles the bleed air and air conditioning controls. I drew it as a vector file with the engraving in one color and the cut lines in another, laying out labels such as RECIRC FAN, ISOLATION VALVE, L PACK, R PACK, WING ANTI ICE and APU BLEED. The two files show the labeled artwork and the matching sheet of cut and engrave paths ready for the laser.
