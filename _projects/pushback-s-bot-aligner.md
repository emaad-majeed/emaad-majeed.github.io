This is an aligner I designed in CAD for the Push Back S Bot, a VEX V5 competition robot. The render is a close view under the front of the chassis, showing the metal rails, omni wheels, and a red gear, with a low plate spanning the frame to line the robot up against field elements and game pieces. I mounted it low and wide so it can register against the field without getting in the way of the wheels. This image is the design view of that subsystem.
