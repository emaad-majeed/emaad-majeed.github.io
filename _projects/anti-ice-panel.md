This is the anti ice panel from the overhead of my Boeing 737 cockpit, carrying the wing anti ice switch and the two engine anti ice switches. I designed the panel face as a laser cut vector, laying out the labels and the off and on positions along with the switch holes, and then cut it. A second drawing is the mounting layer with the cutouts and screw holes that hold the switches and let the face sit in the cockpit. It is one panel in a larger 737 build.
