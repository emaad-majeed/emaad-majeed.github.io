This is High Stakes Robot 1, the first competition robot I built for the VEX V5 High Stakes season under team number 38535C. It runs on a tank tread base with a tall metal arm reaching up over the chassis, driven by a chain and sprocket, and it carries the V5 brain and battery low on the frame. The build photos show it on the workbench during assembly and later on the practice field next to the game rings.
