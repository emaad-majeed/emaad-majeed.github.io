This is a CAD concept I designed for a VEX V5 intake aimed at the Push Back season game ball. The renders show a pair of laser cut side plates carrying rollers, driven through a set of red gears off a single motor cartridge, with a game ball sitting in the mouth of the intake to check the spacing. I shaped the plates so the rollers sit at the right height to grab the ball and feed it back into the robot. These images are a design study rather than a finished build.
