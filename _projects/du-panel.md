This is the display unit selector panel from my homemade 737 cockpit. The laser cut face carries the MAIN PANEL DUs rotary with its OUTBD PFD, NORM, ENG PRI, PFD, MFD and INBD positions, alongside the LOWER DU rotary with ENG PRI, NORM and ND. I designed the labels and the switch openings as a laser cut vector and cut the dark faced panel, then fit rotary switches into the two openings. The install photo shows the panel mounted in the cockpit frame with the red and black wiring running down from the switches at the back side.
