I designed a custom 90 degree gusset to tie together frame members on the Push Back small bot. The bracket braces one side of the robot while also serving as a motor mount.

<dl class="meta">
  <div><dt>Methods</dt><dd>CAD, laser cutting</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Tools</dt><dd>CAD software, laser cutter</dd></div>
</dl>

## 1. Process

<div class="project-step" markdown="1">
<div class="step-copy" markdown="1">

I started with a flat bracket that could connect the frame members and hold the motor mount in place. I then cut pockets into the profile to remove material while leaving a continuous structure around the mounting points.

## 2. Final Design

<div class="project-step final-step" markdown="1">
<div class="step-copy" markdown="1">

The finished robot uses the orange gusset as a visible brace along the side of the frame. Its cutouts keep the bracket open while the solid edges and mounting points connect it back to the surrounding structure.
