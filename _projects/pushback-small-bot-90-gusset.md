I designed a custom 90 degree gusset to tie together frame members on the Push Back small bot. It is a flat bracket with pocketing that takes out weight while keeping the load carrying paths solid, ready to be laser cut. This gusset also doubled as a motor mount for one of the sides of the robot. I cut out a hole for the motor to go through, and the plastic held a mount that the motor was mounted onto.
