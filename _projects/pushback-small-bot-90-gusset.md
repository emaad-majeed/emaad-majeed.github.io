This is a VEX V5 robotics project. I designed a custom 90 degree gusset to tie together frame members on the Push Back small bot. The design screenshots show a flat bracket with a lattice of cutouts that remove weight while keeping the load carrying paths solid, ready to be laser cut. I shaped the internal webs so the part stays stiff at the corner it braces.
