This is a VEX V5 robotics project. I designed a custom 90 degree gusset to tie together frame members on the Push Back small bot. The CAD shows a flat bracket with pocketing that removes weight while keeping the load carrying paths solid, ready to be laser cut. This gusset also doubled as a motor mount for one of the sides of the robot. I cut out a hole for the motor to go through, and the plastic held a mount that the motor was mounted onto.
