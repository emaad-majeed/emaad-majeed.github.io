This is the landing gear panel from my homemade 737 flight simulator cockpit. The face is a tall grey plate with a long slot for the gear lever, engraved OFF and DN positions, and the landing gear and flaps limit speed tables printed below. I mounted it into the main panel structure and wired its switches on the back side by hand so the gear positions could drive Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020.
