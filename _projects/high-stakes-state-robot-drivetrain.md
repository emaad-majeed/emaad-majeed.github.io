This is the drivetrain from my VEX V5 High Stakes state robot, the wheeled base that carries and moves the whole machine. It is geared for 450 RPM on 3.25 inch wheels and runs 6 motors. I built it from aluminum C channel with the wheels driven through gears on each side.
