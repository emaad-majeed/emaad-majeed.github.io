This is the drivetrain from my VEX V5 High Stakes state robot, the wheeled base that carries and moves the whole machine. I built it from aluminum C channel with omni wheels driven through red and green gears and geared motor blocks on each side. The photos show the bare chassis on the bench before the upper mechanisms were added.
