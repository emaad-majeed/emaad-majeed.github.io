This is the electrical bus panel that lives on the overhead of my homemade 737 cockpit. The face carries the BUS TRANSFER switch along with the GEN 1, APU GEN and GEN 2 rotary and switch positions, and I drew each label and cutout as a laser cut vector before cutting the panel. The photos show the cut white panel held up against a darker sub plate, with the switch and knob openings ready for hardware. In the workspace you can also see the wiring, alligator clips and an Arduino board I used while building up the cockpit electronics.
