These are the connectors I used to bundle the wiring behind the 737 cockpit panels. Each metal shielded plug takes a row of individual wires soldered to its pins, so a whole panel can join the rest of the harness through one connection instead of many loose leads. I ran red and white and gray wires to the pins and kept a small circuit board nearby for the signals. Grouping the wires this way made the cockpit easier to take apart and reconnect during the build.
