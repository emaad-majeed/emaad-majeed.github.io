This is the doors panel from the overhead of my homemade Boeing 737 flight simulator cockpit. The laser cut design is a simple rectangular plate with a grid of rectangular cutouts for the door indicator lights and mounting holes at each corner. I cut this so the door status indicators could sit flush in the overhead. Only the vector design file is shown here.
