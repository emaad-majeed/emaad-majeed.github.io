This shows the back side of the overhead panels where I wired everything up. Each switch is hand soldered with resistors and runs through white ribbon wire that carries the signals back toward the Arduino driving Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020. The panels sit in their wooden frame while I route and bundle the wiring behind them.
