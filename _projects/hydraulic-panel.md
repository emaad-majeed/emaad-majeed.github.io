This is the hydraulic panel from my homemade 737 cockpit. It holds the HYD PUMPS switches labelled ENG 1, ELEC 2, ELEC 1 and ENG 2, each with its OFF and ON positions and the A and B system labels. The first design file shows the finished panel artwork with the text in place, and the second shows the raw cut outline for the switch and light openings.
