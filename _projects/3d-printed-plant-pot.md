This is a plant pot I designed in CAD and 3D printed for my Maker Faire biology project, where we made a self watering plant using this pot. The model is a clean cylinder with a rounded interior to hold the plant. I kept the walls simple and the proportions compact so it prints easily and sits neatly on a shelf.
