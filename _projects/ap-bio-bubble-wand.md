This is a bubble wand I designed in CAD for an AP Biology lab where we were trying to simulate cell walls. The model is a flat handle with a round loop at the end that holds a soap film for blowing bubbles. I kept the handle thin and the loop clean so it dips and releases a bubble reliably.
