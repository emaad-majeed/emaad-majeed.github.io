These are some of the overhead panels finished in light and white tones with the switch cutouts and labels engraved into the surface. Panels like the fuel pump and generator sections carry text such as CROSS FEED, FUEL PUMPS, BUS TRANSFER and APU GEN alongside round holes for switches and rectangular openings for indicators. I laid each one out as a vector file and laser cut and engraved it before mounting it into the overhead frame.
