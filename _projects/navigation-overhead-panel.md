This is the navigation overhead panel from my homemade Boeing 737 flight simulator cockpit. I laser cut the panel with the NAVIGATION and DISPLAY labels engraved, along with rotary selector positions for VHF NAV, IRS, FMC, the display source, and the control panel. I drilled and cut the openings for the rotary switches, then mounted and wired them from the back side so the simulator could read each switch position. The build photos show the finished dark panel and how it sits alongside the rest of the overhead stack.
