I designed this VEX V5 drivetrain in CAD as a four wheel base built on a metal C channel frame. The power comes from four black V5 smart motors mounted in the center, and each motor drives a train of red gears that carry torque out to the wheels. The gears are stacked vertically so the ratio fits in a compact footprint, and the setup is geared for 450 RPM. Each corner rides on an omni wheel so the base can turn and strafe smoothly.
