This is the standby power panel from the overhead of my Boeing 737 cockpit, holding the standby power switch with its bat, off, and auto positions and the two disconnect switches numbered one and two. I designed the panel face as a laser cut vector, laying out the labels, the switch and indicator cutouts, and the mounting holes, and then cut it. A second drawing is the mounting layer with matching cutouts and screw holes that back the face and hold the switches. It is one panel in a larger 737 build.
