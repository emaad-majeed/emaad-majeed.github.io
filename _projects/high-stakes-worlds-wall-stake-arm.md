This is the wall stake arm I designed in CAD for my VEX V5 High Stakes World Championship robot. The tall arm rises off the chassis on braced metal rails and carries a ring up to the height of a wall stake so it can be scored, with laser cut Delrin gussets tying the corners together and an omni wheel drivetrain underneath. The render shows the full robot with the arm raised and a ring held near the top.
