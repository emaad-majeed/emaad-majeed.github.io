This is the doinker from my VEX V5 High Stakes state robot, a small pivoting arm that swings out to grab or hold a game element at the edge of the field. I built it from a plastic bar and a short jointed linkage so it could fold in when not in use. The photo shows the doinker arm off the robot on the build bench.
