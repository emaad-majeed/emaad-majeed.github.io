This is the electrical panel from the overhead of my homemade Boeing 737 flight simulator cockpit. The laser cut layout has openings for the DC and AC meters at the top, a DC source selector, an AC source selector, a two position BAT switch, and a GALLEY switch, all with engraved labels split into DC and AC sections. I cut the meter windows and switch holes, then mounted the rotary switches and toggle switches and wired them from the back side. The finished panel has the black meter openings cut and the switches installed.
