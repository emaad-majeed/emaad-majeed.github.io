The AFDS (Autopilot Flight Director System) panel from the 737 overhead.

I designed the panel face as a laser cut vector: an octagonal plate with cutouts for its switches and displays, a two position rotary selector, and an engraved *TEST* label. The red lines are cut paths and the black areas are engraving.

![AFDS panel vector design showing cutouts, rotary selector, and engraved TEST label](/assets/projects/afds-panel/design-01.png)

![AFDS panel design detail](/assets/projects/afds-panel/design-02.png)

![AFDS panel design detail](/assets/projects/afds-panel/design-03.png)

After cutting and painting the panel, I wired its inputs to an Arduino through a hand soldered protoshield so the switches could drive Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020.

![Arduino protoshield and wiring behind the panel](/assets/projects/afds-panel/photo-01.jpg)

![Building and wiring the panel](/assets/projects/afds-panel/photo-02.jpg)

![Building and wiring the panel](/assets/projects/afds-panel/photo-03.jpg)

Finally, the panel installed into the overhead structure alongside the other panels.

![AFDS panel installed in the overhead panel assembly](/assets/projects/afds-panel/photo-04.jpg)
