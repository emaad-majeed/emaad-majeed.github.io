This is the Flight Management Computer keypad from my homemade 737 cockpit. The laser cut vector lays out the full set of keys, including the function row of RTE, CLB, CRZ, MENU, HOLD, FIX, PROG, LEGS, EXEC and DES, the page keys, the numeric pad and the complete A to Z letter grid with SP, DEL, slash and CLR. The photos show the individual black key caps, each with its own printed label such as HOLD, W and R, and the finished keypad assembled into the cockpit frame with a screen opening above it. Building this meant placing and wiring a large number of buttons so the whole keypad could feed the flight simulator.
