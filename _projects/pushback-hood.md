This is a hood plate I designed for a VEX V5 robot built for the Push Back season. The CAD drawing is a flat vector for laser cutting, with mounting holes along the edges and a web of triangular cutouts through the middle that remove material and keep the part light. The build photo shows the hood mounted on the robot as part of the scoring mechanism, wrapping over the top of the ball path. The design pairs a stiff outer frame with the open interior so it holds shape without adding much weight.
