This is the mobile goal clamp from the VEX V5 robot I brought to the High Stakes state competition. It is a top down double direction clamp driven by pistons, so it could clamp in any direction. The whole mechanism packs in among the drive gears, wheels and air tubing, with my team number plate mounted on the frame.
