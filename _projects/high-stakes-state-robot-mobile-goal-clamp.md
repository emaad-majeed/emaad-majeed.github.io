This is the mobile goal clamp from the VEX V5 robot I brought to the High Stakes state competition. The clamp is built into the metal chassis and driven by pneumatic cylinders, so a burst of air swings the clamping arms to grab and hold a mobile goal against the back of the robot. The photos show the mechanism packed in among the drive gears, omni wheels, and air tubing, with my team number plate mounted on the frame.
