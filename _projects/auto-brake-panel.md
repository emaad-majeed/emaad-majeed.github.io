This is a combined forward panel from my Boeing 737 cockpit that groups the speed ref selector, the autobrake selector, the antiskid switch, and the fuel flow controls onto one face. I designed the face as a laser cut vector, with the rotary selector positions, labels, and button and display cutouts laid out, and then cut it. The finished panel came out dark gray with engraved white text, and all of the openings cut clean for the switches and readouts. It is part of a larger 737 cockpit build.
