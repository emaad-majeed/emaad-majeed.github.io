This is a complete VEX V5 competition robot I designed in CAD and built for the Push Back season. The renders show a metal chassis on omni and traction wheels, a scissor style lift carrying an arm, and an intake that feeds the game balls up into the robot, with the balls modeled in red to check clearances. The build photos show the finished machine assembled with its motors, battery, and carbon fiber panels, run under team 38535X Nox. I worked through the frame, the drivetrain, and the scoring path together so the subsystems packaged into one robot.
