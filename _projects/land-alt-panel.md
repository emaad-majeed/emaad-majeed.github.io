This is the land altitude panel from my homemade 737 cockpit, the cabin pressurization control. The design splits into an AUTO side with the FLT ALT and LAND ALT selectors and a MANUAL side with the outflow VALVE control marked OPEN and CLOSE and an ALTN mode selector marked AUTO, ALTN and MAN. The first design file shows the finished panel artwork with all the labels, and the second shows the raw cut outline for the display windows, knobs and rotary openings.
