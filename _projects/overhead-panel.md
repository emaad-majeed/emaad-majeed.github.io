This is the overall overhead panel of my homemade Boeing 737 flight simulator cockpit, where the individual laser cut panels come together into one stack. The photos show the navigation, fuel, bus transfer, ground power, and lower switch panels mounted onto the laser cut wooden frame, with many of the toggle switches and rotary switches already installed and wired. This assembly is what holds all of the smaller overhead panels in place above the cockpit.
