This is the intake from my VEX V5 High Stakes state robot, the mechanism that pulls rings into the robot for scoring. I built it with motor driven rollers, jointed plastic links, and rubber bands that keep tension on the moving parts. The photos show the intake mounted on the robot during assembly and a close view of the tensioned linkage.
