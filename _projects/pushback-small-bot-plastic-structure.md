This is a VEX V5 robotics project. I modeled a custom plastic structural piece for the Push Back small bot, shaped like a C channel with a mounting tab and a grid of square holes for standoffs and screws. The design screenshots show the part from a few angles, both as a wireframe and as a solid render. I used the square hole pattern so it mounts cleanly onto the rest of the metal frame.
