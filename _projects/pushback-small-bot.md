This is a VEX V5 robotics project. I designed this small bot in CAD and then built the physical version for the Push Back season. The design screenshots show the top down layout of the drivetrain, motors, and frame, while the build photo shows the assembled robot with mecanum wheels, a chain driven intake, and the 38535X NOX brain plate. I kept the footprint small so the robot stays nimble on the field.
