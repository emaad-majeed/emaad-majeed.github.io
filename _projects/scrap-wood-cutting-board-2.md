This is a second end grain cutting board I made from scrap wood. I arranged offcuts of different colored wood into a brick like checkerboard, glued them into a block, cut across the end grain, and glued up the final board with rounded corners. The photos show the finished board with the brick pattern.

A demonstration video will be added.
