This is the flight control overhead panel from my homemade 737 cockpit. I laid out the cutouts and labels for the FLT CONTROL A and B switches, the SPOILER A and B switches, the YAW DAMPER switch, the STANDBY HYD switch and the ALTERNATE FLAPS control, each with its OFF, ON and position labels. The first design file shows the finished panel artwork with all the text, and the second shows the raw cut outlines for the switch and rotary openings.
