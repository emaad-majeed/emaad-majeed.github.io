These are the annunciator warning lights for my Boeing 737 cockpit, the small illuminated legends that call out conditions like cabin altitude, takeoff config, and the speedbrake states. I designed the legend faces as a laser cut vector, with each caption boxed so it can be back lit, and then cut them. Behind the face I built a small housing that holds LEDs, and one photo shows a black printed light box with two LEDs and their legs still bent out ready to be wired in. It is part of a larger 737 cockpit build.
